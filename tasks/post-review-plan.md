2. **Trustworthy experimental evaluation.** Bind actual factories, recorded

3. **Trustworthy evidence in one playable case.** Reconcile observation clocks,
   opaque identities, witnessed movement, public death bounds and the relevant

   impossible and insufficient-evidence variants, including semantic plants.
4. **Accusation, answer and vote.** Compare common public account vocabulary,
   retained pretend-task activity, speakable kill testimony and bounded replies.
   Preserve certified vent behavior as the baseline; compare a recorded
   testimony profile whose listeners do not receive another speaker's private
   truth certificate. Own observations remain reliable. No default adoption.
5. **Investigation across rounds.** Add a bounded typed missing-player search
   using last-known observations, then accompaniment if justified. Deterministic
   tactics enforce expiry and urgent-action overrides. Compare context-dependent
   self-report independently. Future observations never prove a past alibi.
6. **Fresh decision evaluation.** Freeze candidate, inputs, comparison rules and
   acceptable tradeoffs before held-out evaluation. Live calls require an
   explicit provider and token, wall-time and cost budget. Publish correct and
   wrongful decisions with coverage, abstention, correction, role exposure,
   information gained, costs and effective changed trajectories. Adopt, revise
   or reject explicitly; offline checks do not establish model reasoning gains.
