### Independent plan review

The viewer/meeting worker compared the draft against
`experiments/deduction_evaluation.py::comparison_arms`, `scenario_cases` and the actual
scripted scenario provider. The seven cases, six independent profile arms and
42 development captures are described accurately. Those custom-policy,
scripted-abstention controls cannot establish spontaneous investigation, model
quality or held-out generalization. The draft preserves prior verdicts, justified
abstention, separate evidence entitlements and explicit spending authorization.

Two actionable findings were accepted by the coordinator and draft author:

1. Fresh ejections can change later participants, tasks and terminal state, so
   fixed legal trajectories cannot remain fixed across multiple fresh meetings.
   The controlled unit is one actual frozen legal prefix plus one fresh meeting.
   Apply its result and stop explicitly; terminal outcomes remain terminal and
   other units are reported as deliberately partial. Later boundaries require
   their own independently frozen prefixes. Complete downstream divergence
   belongs to the normal-policy evaluation.
2. Knowing hidden roles while grading evidence support risks rewarding a lucky
   correct guess. First grade support from the deciding agent's entitled inputs
   without hidden roles or full trajectories, then grade truth/role accuracy
   separately. Freeze the rubric, grader process and disagreement handling before
   confirmation. Judge information never returns to a listener or tactic.

These are prospective design corrections, not new measurements or alterations
to historical records. The source/input freeze, justified numeric decision bars,
sample sizes and provider budget remain execution prerequisites. The author
handed the matching preregistration changes to the reviewer, who applied both
corrections. `.venv/bin/python scripts/validate_task_docs.py` passed with 390
historical tasks/prompts and 33 work cards after the changes. The coordinator's
combined project gate remains pending; no live evaluation was executed.
