## Headline

The round-2 reporter flag (17/114 = 0.149) is mostly an old reporter bias, made larger by the switch from vent proof to deduction. Reporters take about 3/4 of innocent ejections while holding about 1/4 of innocent seats, the same as at baseline 9. Within it, the process failure is route arithmetic: in 11 of 22 innocent ejections the table called a walkable route impossible. That includes all 5 reporters who watched the kill and named the killer, then were ejected. This is a limit of the 27B model, and the one existing route check (off, one hop only) would not catch the witness cases. A narrow round 3 testing a recorded route-check arm is warranted.

## Recommendations

- A round 3 is warranted, but aimed at route reasoning, not the reporter seat. Keep the same 8 arms, kill_cooldown_ticks 6, seeds 0-49 and the same ceilings. Add one recorded, config-only, default-off meeting-layer field with its own stamp, like ballot_kill_row_version (for example route_reconciliation_version 1). Never use the ambient toggles corroboration_discipline or testimony_shapes.
- What the arm renders: on every ballot and on the bounded-reply prompt, one role-blind line per living candidate. It names the pairs of places stated at the table about that candidate that the map can link within the elapsed ticks (hops no more than ticks). It covers the candidate's own whereabouts and alibi legs, others' move sightings, and walks of several hops. It never asserts presence or honesty. Before any spend, prove it with planted cases: West Hall to Admin (1 hop, 1 tick), Admin to Cafeteria (2, 2), Cafeteria to Storage (3, 3). Then run the fake and scripted rehearsals.
- Pre-register for round 3: innocent ejections whose ejected player had a stated pair the map links (r2: 12/22); lone kill-witness outcomes (r2: the witness ejected 5 of 9); ejection per seat for reporters, innocent non-reporters and impostors, split by vent proof; and the impostor win share against the 0.20-0.60 envelope (r2: 0.48). An arm that saves witnesses favours the crew, so watch the 0.20 floor.
- Do not spend the round's one dial on: an opener-only reply value (at most 2 of 17 cases); the kill-time information (removing it was the B4 arm's purpose, and it matches the genre); or the strategic impostor ballot (genre behaviour that amplifies crew errors rather than causing them).
- If the owner prefers no further spend: read the flag against its base rate, take the era-keyed promotion of round 2, and state the route errors as the 27B model's limit on the shown set. Round 2's pre-registered verdict stands as recorded.
- Census additions, so the flag reads with its base rate: (1) a table of ejection per seat at report meetings (reporter, innocent non-reporter, impostor), split by vent proof, with a relative flag in place of the absolute 0.104; (2) what happened to the witness of a witnessed kill, lone versus corroborated (killer ejected, witness ejected, other, skip); (3) ejections of a player who had a stated pair the map links; (4) ejections the impostor ballots decided, read both as SKIP and as removed, next to the floor-only cell; (5) impostor EJECTs citing a turn that does not point toward the target (r2: 45/111), plus who got the reply when an accused opener did not.
