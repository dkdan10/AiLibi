### 2.1 The same-seed scripted-FSM comparator

The comparator for clause (a) is **the same-substrate FSM comparator row recorded on the
same seeds at 18.26** (`tasks/phase-18.md` 18.26: "impostor finalists against the
scripted-FSM crew (the §1.3 comparator discipline: the same-seed FSM row re-recorded if
the substrate moved)" — the quoted §1.3 is `audits/audit-phase-17-close.md` §1.3 — and
its DoD: "with the same-substrate FSM comparator row recorded on the same seeds"). Five
consequences, stated now so no consumer improvises:

- **18.17 records candidates only** — its re-rank recorder never emits FSM rows. The
  comparator bytes come from 18.26 (or, where a campaign report wants an interim
  same-seed read, from an explicitly recorded FSM arm with the same provenance
  discipline); a claim is never judged against a comparator that was not recorded on the
  same seeds at the same substrate.
- **The corpus is the anchor denominator, not the claim comparator.** The §3/§4 cells
  are the committed baseline the campaign sweeps quote deltas against (18.24 DoD); the
  CLAIM at 18.27 is ruled on the 18.26 same-seed comparator arm. Where the substrate
  never moves, the corpus and the comparator are same-substrate populations and §5 shows
  they agree cell-by-cell (one flagged exception).
- **Real path** means the recorded real-provider path (the 17.14 recorder discipline,
  stamps read back from bytes) — fake-path sweeps inform, but clause (a) is a real-path
  read (`audits/audit-phase-18-planning.md` §5: "a named instrument delta vs the
  same-seed scripted-FSM comparator on the real path").
- **Which anchor column a sweep quotes against:** a campaign sweep (18.24/18.25) quotes
  its deltas against the CORPUS column matching the recording's roster shape (a 9p2i leg
  → the corpus-9p2i cells). Where that column has no pinned cell (corpus-4p1i
  kill-craft, §4.1; the unpinned corpus-4p1i deflection subcells, §3.5) or a None/0-0
  cell (the corpus-4p1i vouch rates, §3.1), the sweep quotes the arm's raw cell with its
  Wilson interval and NO delta — the samples column is a continuity cross-check, never
  the anchor.
- **Crew claims are judged opponent-matched:** a crew claim cell's candidate arm is
  (crew finalist vs opponent O) and its comparator is (scripted-FSM crew vs the same O,
  same seeds, same substrate) — 18.26 records a scripted-crew comparator row for EACH
  opponent a crew finalist is recorded against (the scripted impostor and, where used,
  the frozen impostor champion), or the crew claim on that opponent pairing reads
  NOT-DEMONSTRATED for want of a comparator.

## 6. THE FOUR-PART CLAIM DISCIPLINE **[PROPOSED — ratified at merge]**

The contract's discipline, verbatim (`tasks/phase-18.md`, Task 18.4; identical in
substance to `audits/audit-phase-18-planning.md` §5):

> a behavior counts as EMERGENT only if (a) its instrument delta vs the same-seed
> scripted-FSM comparator on the real path is significant at |z| ≥ 1.96 on the
> pre-registered denominator; (b) the delta reproduces across at least 2 of the 3 corpus
> seed-splits; (c) a named counterfactual ablation (remove the enabling lever/feature)
> shows the behavior recede; (d) the behavior is selected-for — present in the champion's
> recordings, not only the archive. Watchability improvement is never itself an emergence
> claim.

The four clauses are **conjunctive**: failing any one reads NOT-DEMONSTRATED — there is
no "partially emergent". The exact rules per clause:

### 6.a Significance — the exact statistical rules

**Rate cells** (every cell in §2.2 whose claim cell is a numerator/denominator pair): the
**pooled two-proportion z**, the standing convention of the phase-16/17 close canaries:

```
z = (p̂_c − p̂_f) / sqrt( p̂ (1 − p̂) (1/n_c + 1/n_f) )
p̂_c = k_c/n_c   p̂_f = k_f/n_f   p̂ = (k_c + k_f) / (n_c + n_f)
```

with subscript c = the candidate/champion arm and f = the same-seed FSM comparator arm
(§2.1), both numerators and denominators being the registered report fields (§2.2), fired
two-sided at **|z| ≥ 1.96**. When the pooled rate is 0 or 1 (both arms at the same
extreme) the z is undefined and no delta exists — the honest no-claim read, not a pass.
An arm whose registered DENOMINATOR field is 0 on the claim cell (reachable — the
committed corpus-4p1i vouch denominators are 0 today) admits no z at all: no delta
exists and the claim reads NOT-DEMONSTRATED as recorded (the remedy is more seeds, not a
waiver).

**The correlation cell** (`kill-craft` within-one-hop point-biserial): **Fisher r-to-z**,

```
z = (atanh r_c − atanh r_f) / sqrt( 1/(n_c − 3) + 1/(n_f − 3) )
```

with n = the per-kill row count (`kills_total`), |z| ≥ 1.96. An arm whose correlation is
None (zero variance — e.g. the committed co-present cell) admits no correlation claim on
that cell; the departure is claimed through the co-present-kill RATE cell instead
(§4.1).

**Mean cells** (`action-entropy` per-side mean conditional entropy; the witness-density
means where quoted): the **two-sample Welch z** over the per-unit values,

```
z = (m_c − m_f) / sqrt( s_c²/N_c + s_f²/N_f )
```

with s² the sample variance with the N−1 divisor, |z| ≥ 1.96, and the unit values
sourced from the committed report — never recomputed outside it:

- **density means** (N = kills): the per-kill unit values ARE emitted — every
  `KillCraftReport.per_kill` row carries `co_present_crew` / `one_hop_crew`, so the
  Welch inputs are computable from the committed report directly.
- **entropy cells** (N = `agents`): the unit is the per-agent conditional entropy, which
  the module computes deterministically but does NOT emit —
  `ActionEntropyCells` carries `agents` / `decisions` / the two means / pooled `buckets`
  only, so s² is not recoverable from committed outputs today. This is a **named
  instrument gap routed back as a contract** (the 18.4 scope rule: eval/ defects found
  at pre-registration route back as contracts, never edits at this gate): a follow-up
  adds the per-agent entropy vector (or its N−1 variance) to `ActionEntropyCells` with
  re-pins, landing BEFORE the campaigns record (the §1 substrate discipline). Adding the
  field is cell plumbing, not a rules amendment — the rule above already names the unit;
  the new field's baseline values re-anchor mechanically with provenance like any
  re-pin. **Until that field is committed and pinned, an entropy claim is unjudgeable
  from committed outputs and reads NOT-DEMONSTRATED as recorded** — an improvised
  out-of-report recomputation never substitutes.

The campaign/finalist report quotes the computed z with its inputs (means, variances,
Ns) beside it — the 15.18 convention.

The DoD names the rate rule; the Fisher and Welch extensions are registered here so the
non-rate Tier-B cells are falsifiable rather than unjudgeable — without them the
correlation and entropy registrations would be decorative **[PROPOSED]**.

### 6.b Split-reproducibility — the exact rule

Partition BOTH arms' recorded games by the `seed mod 5` rule (§2.3: {0,1,2} / {3} / {4})
over their own seed lists. Compute the per-split delta on the same claim cell (same
fields, per-split denominators). **Clause (b) holds iff the pooled delta's SIGN
reproduces in at least 2 of the 3 splits.** Per-split significance is NOT required. A
split with an empty or zero denominator on either arm does not count as reproducing; if
two or more splits are empty, clause (b) is unmet as recorded (the remedy is more seeds,
not a waiver). A per-split delta of exactly 0 — including the both-arms-zero-numerator
case — does not count as reproducing a nonzero pooled sign; the intended consequence is
stated plainly: a departure concentrated in a single seed-split cannot pass (b). For the
correlation cell the per-split delta is r_c − r_f with `kills_total` as the emptiness
test and a None correlation (zero variance) on either arm disqualifying that split; for
mean cells the per-split delta is m_c − m_f with `agents` (entropy) / kills (density) as
the emptiness test.

Priced both directions: requiring per-split |z| ≥ 1.96 at the 10–30-game split scale
would need effects the phase-17 close's own band arithmetic shows are >20 pp — a bar that
deadletters the discipline (nothing real could ever pass); accepting 1-of-3 sign
agreement would let a single-split artifact ride the pooled read. 2-of-3 sign agreement
is the cross-run synchronized-onset bar of the planning dossier (§5, from the
reward-hacking literature) at reachable power: the pooled |z| ≥ 1.96 carries
significance, the splits carry reproducibility.

### 6.c The counterfactual ablation — the naming convention and the recede criterion

**Naming convention (what "18.4-named" means in the 18.24/18.25 DoDs):** every candidate
emergence behavior a campaign report surfaces MUST name its ablation as

```
ablation:<instrument-key>/<lever-id>
```

where `<instrument-key>` is the registered key from §2.2 (`false-vouch`, `frame`,
`teammate-immunity`, `alibi-survival`, `deflection`, `kill-craft`, `off-menu`,
`action-entropy`) and `<lever-id>` names the SINGLE enabling lever/term/feature the
ablation disables, in the campaign configuration's own vocabulary. The lever plane, by
example (not exhaustive — the campaign names what it actually used): `conviction-term`
(the 18.16 reward-side term), `anchor-lambda=<value>` (the 18.5 anchor weight, reverted
to the committed λ=1.0), `pfsp-sampler` (18.20), `exploiter-probe` (18.21), `encoder-v3`
/ `within-kind-targets` (18.22), `scenario/<name>` (18.23), and any adopted meeting-layer
flag (`roll-call`, `endpoint-band`, `impostor-answer`). Parsing: the instrument-key never
contains a slash — parsers split on the FIRST `/` after the `ablation:` prefix and treat
the remainder, slashes included, as the lever-id.

**The run:** identical configuration except the named lever disabled/reverted, same seed
list where the path permits; **fake-path re-runs suffice where the behavior is tactical**
(the 18.24 DoD wording, verbatim); real-path is required where the behavior lives in the
meeting layer. For a **training-time lever** (the conviction term, the anchor weight,
the sampler, the encoder — a recorded genome cannot be evaluated "with the lever off"),
the ablation re-runs the enabling training/selection leg with the lever disabled under
otherwise identical config and training seeds, takes THAT leg's champion, and records it
on the champion arm's recording seed list. For an **eval-time lever** (an adopted
meeting-layer flag), disabling the lever on a re-record of the same policy suffices.
**Provenance:** the ablation run's config, substrate sha, seed list, and
recording/report location are recorded in the campaign report beside the candidate — the
18.27 reading consumes ablation evidence from there and never regenerates it.

**The recede criterion:** the behavior recedes iff, on the ablated arm, BOTH hold on the
SAME claim cell vs the comparator: (i) the delta is no longer significant under §6.a, AND
(ii) the effect itself has receded — the ablated arm's absolute effect is at most HALF
the champion arm's on the cell's own scale: |p̂_abl − p̂_f| ≤ ½·|p̂_c − p̂_f| for rate
cells, |atanh r_abl − atanh r_f| ≤ ½·|atanh r_c − atanh r_f| for the correlation cell,
|m_abl − m_f| ≤ ½·|m_c − m_f| for mean cells — with the ablated arm's denominator still
at least HALF the champion arm's (an underpowered ablation read is not a recede; it is an
unfinished ablation). Why (ii) is load-bearing, priced with a concrete counterexample
**[INFERRED — §10]**: significance loss alone counts LOST POWER as recession — a
champion arm at 23/59 vs a 13/59 comparator fires (z = 2.00, effect +0.170), and an
ablated arm at 12/30 vs the same comparator clears the ½-denominator floor (30 ≥ 29.5)
and reads z = 1.78 < 1.96 while its effect GREW to +0.180; under (i) alone that would
record a recede with the behavior fully intact. Requiring a full return to the
comparator would over-demand in the other direction (noise alone leaves residue at these
Ns); half-effect is the registered midpoint. Provenance is never mixed across the
comparison: a real-path ablation is judged against the 18.26 real-path comparator; a
fake-path ablation is judged against a same-seed fake-path FSM arm recorded beside it
with the same provenance discipline, with the un-ablated candidate's fake-path delta
quoted beside the pair so the recede is like-for-like. **An unablated candidate reads
NOT-DEMONSTRATED by construction** (the 18.24 contract sentence, carried verbatim).

### 6.d Selected-for — the surface

Clause (d) is judged on the surfaces of §2.4: the delta must be present in the champion's
own recordings (the 18.26 finalist arm — which is also the arm clause (a) is judged on,
so a clause-(a) pass on the champion's arm satisfies (d) by construction), not only in
archive members or non-champion entrants. A behavior that lives only in MAP-Elites cells
or hall-of-fame entries is an archive finding — reportable, never EMERGENT.

### The watchability exclusion

Verbatim, from the contract and the planning §5: **"Watchability improvement is never
itself an emergence claim"** — watchability is a gate, not a target
(`audits/audit-phase-18-planning.md` §5: "'Watchability improved' is never itself the emergence
claim (gate, not target)"). No watchability score, floor margin, or referee verdict
appears as a claim cell in §2.2, and none may be added except through §9.
