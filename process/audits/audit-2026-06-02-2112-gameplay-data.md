## 8. Improvement proposals

### gp-imp-1 — Wire fellow_impostor_ids into the meeting speech/vote path (Wave 2 / J-5)

- **Findings:** D-D-1, D-D-2, D-D-3, D-D-4
- **Scope sketch:** The firewall is delivered on the self channel (`observation/service.py:101-111`) and consumed by the kill policy (`impostor_policy.py:43-48`) but NOT by meeting generation; `agents/perception.py:196` explicitly defers this to "Wave 2 (J-5)". Hard-exclude `fellow_impostor_ids` from an impostor's accusation targets and ballot candidates the same way kill candidates already are; optionally emit teammate corroboration. **Reproduce:** seed 6, meeting-0 at tick 8 — impostor p-6 votes p-6→p-3 (its teammate), supplying the pivotal 2nd vote of a 2-vote plurality; with that vote redirected the tie (1-1-1-1) SKIPS by `meetings/voting.py` rule 3 and impostor p-3 survives. Also seed 49 meeting-0 (p-6→p-5) and the 11 mutual-betrayal meetings (D-D-2). **Regression assertions (no role inference needed):** mutual-betrayal-meeting count → 0; impostor-betrayal-ballot rate (currently 40/91) → ~0; report-accusation-vs-teammate (48/54) → 0; impostor→teammate corroboration (0/19) > 0.
- **Priority:** pre-wave-1 — this is Wave-2 scope by the roadmap, but it is the single largest distortion of the current baseline: the 68% impostor win and 5/5 "correct" impostor ejections are inflated by self-sabotage, so Wave-1 crew-intel A/B will be measured against a corrupted impostor baseline unless this is at least understood (and ideally landed) first.

### gp-imp-2 — Crew-intel: convert flagged contradictions into convictions; fix the threat model

- **Findings:** C-C-1, C-C-2, C-C-3, C-C-4, B-B-3, F-F-6, F-F-7
- **Scope sketch:** The dominant crew failure is crew-on-crew false accusation over noisy alibi-conflict signals, not impostor cascades (C-C-1). Contradiction count is near-noise (47% lands on crew; most-flagged is impostor only 59% — C-C-2) and is ignored anyway (34/37 SKIPPED meetings carried contradictions — B-B-3/F-F-6). The §4.6 confidence-coupled 0.55-SKIP enum makes vote confidence uninformative (C-C-3). **Reproduce:** seed 0, meeting-0 — 5 contradiction flags fire on crewmate p-4 over a copy-paste "CAFETERIA ticks 0-7" alibi 4 players gave verbatim; crew p-2,p-7 eject p-4 (CREW) on a 2-vote plurality across 4 targets, no impostor vote on the victim → impostor win. Seed 49, meeting-0 at tick 6 — crewmate p-3 carries 8 flags vs impostor p-5's 2, count anti-correlated with guilt. **Levers:** suppress self-reported alibis as a contradiction source / weight by independent corroboration (C-C-2); decouple confidence from the skip decision in `vote_ballot.j2` (C-C-3); require a plurality margin or distinct-accuser quorum before ejection (C-C-4). **Target metric:** `contradictions_flagged_but_ignored` (34 → down) and `ejection_accuracy` (0.556 → up), NOT the headline win %.
- **Priority:** pre-wave-1 — this IS the gp-4 crew-intel work; the proposals here scope what to target (false-accusation threat model + ignored-contradiction conversion) before the Wave-1 PR is written.

### gp-imp-4 — Deterministic SKIP short-circuit to cap meeting token waste

- **Findings:** E-E-2, E-E-4
