## 7. Metric soundness, coverage & balance

- **F-F-1 / B-B-7 (high): `meeting_rate` denominator mismatch.** The facts-JSON aggregate reports `meeting_rate=0.9` = total_meetings(45)/games(50); the canonical `eval.meeting_quality.compute_meeting_rate` defines it as games_with_meeting/games_total = 37/50 = **0.74** (matching the MEMORY baseline for this same HEAD). The Stage-A gate (≥0.60) passes either way, so this is reporting-correctness, not a gate flip — but a reviewer trusting the facts aggregate would publish the wrong headline and Wave-1 before/after comparisons against the 0.74 baseline would compare against the wrong denominator. **Fix the Extract step or rename the field to `meetings_per_game`.**
- **F-F-2 (medium, verifier-downgraded from high): `vote_correctness_rate=1.0` is statistically empty (n=2).** Only 2 impostor ejections set-wide, both evidence-backed → 1.0 with `vote_correctness_small_n=True`. The honest full-denominator companion is `ejection_accuracy=0.40` (2 impostor / 5 total; 3 innocent crew). Sound BY DESIGN (the module flags small_n and ships the companion), but the ejection channel is too thin to support any Wave-1 A/B on this rate.
- **F-F-3 (high): Both calibration ECEs rest on a near-constant quantized confidence enum.** Ballot ECE=0.43 (low_power, 3 bins, 0.85 alone = 45/68 = 66%, impostor-hit flat 0.42–0.44). Claim ECE=0.20 is reported `low_power=False` yet is also degenerate (0.5 and 0.6 cover 94/128 = 73%). The `MIN_POPULATED_BINS_FOR_POWER=5` threshold lets the claim curve through despite identical clustering. **Do not gate any Wave-1 A/B on a calibration ECE delta from this provider** — a delta between two clustered enums is noise. Consider flagging any curve whose modal bin holds >50% of samples.
- **F-F-4 (medium): `contradictions_flagged_but_ignored=36` is the real, adequately-powered deduction signal.** 36 of 40 SKIPPED meetings carried a flagged contradiction the table then ignored (detection works: 341 contradictions, 41/45 meetings flagged ≥1; conversion fails: 5 total ejections, 0.11/meeting). **Promote this to a primary Wave-1 crew-intel metric (n=36) and gate on driving it down**, paired with `ejection_accuracy` (n=5) — not `vote_correctness_rate` (n=2).
- **F-F-5 (medium): Balance is impostor-skewed (74%) and crew wins are pure task-races.** 12 of 13 crew wins had ZERO impostor ejections; impostors ejected in only 2 of 50 games. The crew-win channel carries no deduction signal, so a crew-intel improvement that votes better would barely move the win split. **Do not read 74/26 as a deduction-balance baseline — it is a kill-speed-vs-task-speed race baseline.**
- **F-F-6 (low): Coverage is healthy except three degenerate channels.** Kills/game 1–4 (median 3), game length 8–25 ticks (median 16), kills across all 8 rooms, meetings/game {0:13, 1:29, 2:8}. Degenerate by construction: `emergency_meetings=0` (the emergency-button pathway is entirely dead — all 45 meetings are crew body-reports), 5 ejections across 50 games, and 13/50 games reach no meeting. Meeting-derived metrics (calibration, alibi, vote) are computed over only 37 meeting-bearing games / 45 meetings — effective n smaller than the 50-game headline.
- **F-F-7 (informational): All USD fields are $0 (Ollama).** Cost cannot anchor the DESIGN §10.4 ~$0.20/game target; token totals (1,670,459 in / 107,952 out) are the intended proxy and fully capture LLM spend for this meeting-only set.
- **F-F-8 (informational): `alibi_fabrication survival_rate=0.40` is a documented conservative lower-bound.** Well-powered (168 alibis / 45 meetings) but by design over-attributes catches, pushing survival down. Read as "at least 40% survive"; prefer comparing absolute survived/total counts across runs and be skeptical of sub-0.05 rate deltas (within the join's documented noise).
