## 0. Verdict in brief

- **Merge with all candidates OFF: yes, after one small correction batch.** The nine required items from the previous review are all resolved, eight of them with adverse tests that fail when the fix is reverted. Every gate is green at HEAD (section 2). Default-path gameplay is unchanged: state-hash sequences are identical to `main` and to `9b333a76` on every seed checked. Four small, cheap items should land first (section 5); the largest is an introduced regression that makes an interrupted tournament permanently unresumable.
- **Adopt any candidate: no, not on this evidence.** The candidate stack (temporal v2, evidence v2, public accounts, attributed testimony, bounded reply, bounded investigation, contextual self-report) is default-OFF and correctly labelled MECHANICS_ONLY. Nothing committed shows any new channel changing a decision: every ballot in both committed captures is SKIP by construction, and a mechanical probe confirms that with the captures' fixed neutral turn no channel is reachable in any arm. Two verified defects starve the reasoner the candidate is built for: the evidence-v2 renderer evicts witnessed vents and most other-player sightings at the default budget (NG3-1 and VENT-2, filed high, confirmed 3/3 in both rounds, on real games), and the same renderer lets speaker-controlled "Account uncertainty" lines crowd out impossible-travel verdicts (GR-1). The accounts detector lets one speaker's own claims mint a conflict against an innocent (NC2-2 and FU-ALIBI-4, confirmed 3/3 twice, with consequences larger than first filed). Attributed mode deliberately removes the shared vent certificate; that is disclosed in the branch's gameplay review and is a design consequence to weigh, not a defect.
- **The branch does not claim the gameplay is better, and this review found no evidence that it is.** Passing tests, checked boxes and the implementation's own checkpoints were treated as claims and re-derived; where they held, this report says so; where they did not (NG1-1, NG1-2, FU-D3, P-02, P-03, and the prose scope of P-01), it says that. Several of this review's own strongest round-2 theses were also overturned by the refuters (that the new channels reduce actionable evidence, that the attributed arm renders no evidence, that the harnesses cannot run a real model) and those refutations are recorded in section 12 because they bear on the adoption question.
- **Correction quality:** the correction batch was competent and narrow. It introduced four new default-path problems (NC4-1 unresumable tournament; FU-2 writer/test projection split; FU-B-01 stale warm cache on one filename class; FU-D2 recording-envelope widening), one new concurrency hazard inside a disclaimed area (CONC-2), and did not adopt or decline any of the previous review's seven workflow recommendations. Complexity grew materially (section 9).

## 8. The vent-detector problem, honestly

The previous review's section 8 defined the problem: in the 9p2i corpus a grounded vent sighting is the only evidence that makes a meeting decide (68/68 eject with one; the rest mostly skip). The candidate adds five channels (source-tick perception, clock-corrected evidence lines, role-blind public accounts, attributed cross-meeting testimony, one bounded reply) and one behaviour (bounded investigation with contextual self-report). This section separates what the evidence supports from what it does not, including where this review's own probes overreached.

**What the committed evidence can and cannot say.** Both committed captures are produced by scripted providers whose meeting turns are fixed: 144 of 144 and 238 of 238 ballots are SKIP by construction. They establish mechanics (the channel exists, the reader reconstructs it, the flags fire, each channel's presence per arm is recorded) and nothing about judgment. The harness labels them MECHANICS_ONLY, which is correct. The five investigation development seeds were selected from 32 candidates for the properties they show, and one (seed 1) was added because it produced the single positive contextual self-report row (NC6-8); they are not held out. On five unseen seeds the search arm's first body report comes later in every case (NG1-2).

**Reachability, mechanically.** A prompt-text-only scripted voter run over six arms, two rosters and five fresh seeds (section 12.1) shows: with the captures' fixed neutral turn, no channel is reachable in any arm; with speech, contradiction lines render in every arm. This review's first reading of that matrix, that each new channel lowers actionable evidence, was refuted: the probe's speaker was tuned to the default rendering. With an arm-independent speaker the new arms render about five times the contradiction content of OFF (192 lines versus 35 on the same 18 meetings), only the attributed arms render an explicit negative route verdict ("the public route is longer than the available interval"), and a voter that cites a public turn and its own observation ejects the true impostor in the attributed arm. Reachability is therefore not the blocker. Model judgment is untested, and two renderer defects (NG3-1/VENT-2, GR-1) would starve it if it were tested today.

**Vent detectability end to end.** In the most favourable scenario (two crewmates witness the same impostor at both ends of a one-tick vent) the vent is caught under the arms the branch is not proposing: evidence v1 mints two strong `vent_sighting` flags plus `alibi_vs_physical`; lever-OFF mints the same flags dated one tick late, the clock defect temporal v2 genuinely repairs. Under the flagship arm the certificate is off by design and the table receives three weak rows ending "An unseen vent is not excluded"; the witness's own vent line survives in a small game but ranks 15th of 30 rendered lines and is gone at budget 800; on realistic games at the default budget it is evicted (VENT-2). A spoken `saw_vent` mints no placement for the listener's travel check (VENT-3). A vent observation feeding the claim branch can produce a degenerate same-room check rendered as "a walk fits the public map", self-disclaimed and not vent-specific (VENT-4).

**Useful evidence versus extra activity.** The investigation lever verifiably changes world trajectories (trajectory hashes differ; movement roughly doubles; task actions fall 20–35 %). Its measured product is more `saw_player` and `saw_player_move` deliveries. Whether it produces more usable evidence is unmeasured: the arm comparison is cross-world, the body rows are per-tick dwell rows, distinct discoveries are 19 versus 15, and one body was found four ticks earlier under search. The accounts and attributed arms change no world trajectory in the scripted matrix; transcripts are byte-identical across arms and only the detector differs. Under a real model they would change speech; that is unmeasured.

**Changed submitted actions versus changed world trajectories.** Correctly distinguished by the investigation harness (both are recorded). The witnessed-kill flight override fires once across the 35 measured games.

**Supported inference versus lucky guess.** Nothing on the branch measures it. The ballot schema already carries `primary_reason_id` and `primary_reason_observation_id`, and a refuter's citing voter used them, so a "supported" check (the cited turn or observation exists in the voter's prompt) is automatable; a privileged truth grader for "and it was right for that reason" does not exist and has to be built and frozen before any fresh-model run. A model that ejects because the death-tick handle (NG3-2) narrows the window is not deducing what the design wants to reward, and the current metrics could not tell.

**A later observation must never certify an earlier alibi.** Across seven authored scenarios and six arms (section 12.5), no rendered line asserts a subject's position at a tick the observer did not record, with one exception on the evidence-v1 path (FU-ALIBI-1). Every "fits the public walking map" line carries a non-innocence disclaimer; death bounds are conservative and cannot regress; the impostor's own kill is excluded from its own discovery bound; the per-event "immediately before" clause is correct; `assess_travel` matches the one-move-per-tick rule; a walk-feasible lie is not endorsed and a distant one is caught; v2 never lets a later claim overwrite an earlier interval; a minted public conflict does not move belief scalars. Evidence v1 with a hub regroup does render a false accusation (FU-ALIBI-2).

**What is structurally missing for deduction to reach a decision.**
1. A decision procedure: one turn per player, no elicited chain of inference. Absence is representable only as a private scalar (`beliefs.py:276`) and is never rendered as text; the reporter is exempt from the absence lift.
2. A renderer that keeps the evidence (NG3-1/VENT-2, GR-1) and says when it has shed something (GR-6).
3. A detector that cannot be gamed by the cheapest lie (NG3-5, NC2-2/FU-ALIBI-4, NG2-2) or turned against a teammate (NC2-3).
4. A measured trade for the certificate: attributed mode buys role-blind accounts at the price of the one hard proof channel; the trade is disclosed but unquantified.

**What is genuinely new and worth keeping.** Conditional public route conflicts ("unseen vent not excluded"), death-time bounds independent of discovery, role-blind task accounts, attributed cross-meeting claims, one bounded reply, and explicit negative route verdicts are real information channels that did not exist before, and mechanically they are reachable. Whether a model can use them is the open question, and section 11 proposes the smallest evaluation that can answer it.

- *Instrument:* a new one, as the preregistration requires. The two committed harnesses are stage-1 MECHANICS_ONLY instruments whose provider refusals are their own acceptance criteria and must stay. - *Arms:* two, paired on identical prefixes: `repaired_clock` (temporal 2, evidence 2, nothing else) versus `combined_accounts` (plus public accounts and attributed testimony). - *Preregister before running:* the prefix inventory and its hashes, the two arms, sampling settings, the primary outcome per unit (correct ejection of the impostor; wrongful ejection counted against), a McNemar test on the paired discordant units with a stated minimum actionable effect (the probe used 0.20 → 0.55, which needs 40–50 units per arm at α 0.05 and power 0.80; no base rate exists to power from, since all committed ballots are SKIP), a "supported" secondary (the ballot's `primary_reason_id` or `primary_reason_observation_id` names a turn or observation present in that voter's prompt), a frozen privileged grader for "right for that reason", and a stop rule.
- *What counts as a result:* if neither arm produces a supported correct ballot, that is a bounded negative on reachability by this model and no larger run is justified. If the candidate arm does and the baseline does not, that justifies a held-out live evaluation (seeds ≥ 100, 7p2i, 20 games per arm, post-hoc exclusion of games where direct proof reached a living crew member). At this size the design cannot show a small effect; it is a screening run and must be described as one.
- *Preconditions:* NG3-1/GR-1 fixed (otherwise the candidate arm is evaluated with its evidence evicted), NC4-3 and NC6-2 fixed (so the arms are labelled and the reconstructed memories are the ones the model saw), and the death-tick handle question (NG3-2) either left as-is and stated, or masked in both arms equally.
