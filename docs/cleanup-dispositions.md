Four dispositions, and every id gets exactly one:

- **Repaired** names the commit that repaired it. A commit, not a green test: a
  passing suite is not by itself evidence that the filed defect is gone.
- **Routed** names the card that owns it. Those cards are in flight; a routed row
  states ownership, never that the work is finished.
- **Retained** states the reason and the trigger that would reopen it.
- **Refuted** states the reasoning, and applies where this tree or the review's
  own later pass contradicts the finding as written, or could not reproduce it at
  the commit that pass reviewed. Not reproducible is a form of refuted rather
  than a fifth word: it says the trigger did not fire for the lens that looked,
  never that the underlying behaviour was repaired.

disagrees with a filed severity or claim, the disagreement is written here beside
