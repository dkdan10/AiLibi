### Ideas (concrete)
1. **Fix the fabricated-completion bug**: track completions from `TaskCompleted` events (or the instance's `completed` flag) instead of pending-id changes; and spread `redistribute_dead_tasks` across crew (round-robin / nearest) instead of "lowest-id".
2. **Render self-location**: `[tick T] You were in ROOM (moved from X)` rows, and pre-fill the roll-call `whereabouts` from the record instead of asking the LLM to guess; reject a whereabouts that contradicts the agent's own record before it reaches the detector.
3. **Add a `moved` observation shape** (`{"type":"moved","tick":T,"subject":X,"from":A,"to":B}`) and make `alibi_vs_sighting` tolerant of the boundary tick (sighting in A at T when subject says B at T and A at T-1 is CONSISTENT). Downgrade single-tick self-placement mismatches to weak unless two independent witnesses agree.
4. **Give voters a map** (adjacency list + travel ticks) and a **time-of-death estimate** (body age band) so "impossible travel" and "who could reach STORAGE between t12 and t17" become checkable; render "body found with X, Y present" as one line.
5. **Make testimony count**: carry independent-witness corroboration into suspicion (two crew placing a player at the scene ≈ a flag), record verified vent testimony as `VERIFIED` in listeners' memory, and store meeting outcomes (`p-3 EJECTED at meeting 1 (was IMPOSTOR/CREW)`); drop the "always restate a held vent" rule once its subject is out.
6. **Impostor FSM**: no vent within N ticks of a kill unless the destination room is empty (impostors can see adjacent rooms — use it); prefer walking away; ban the t0–3 spawn-camp (start a pretend task elsewhere); allow the "linger at the body" only when a teammate can vouch. Give the impostor reply prompt 2–3 deflection templates plus permission to state its own (fake) whereabouts, so the "you rushed to the scene" tell is not universal.
7. **Meeting protocol/world**: clear all bodies and gather players in CAFETERIA at meeting end (or at least don't let a known-dead corpse open a fresh meeting); add a 2–3 tick post-meeting kill cooldown; forbid vent-enter on the meeting tick; give finished crew a follow/escort/patrol behaviour and enable sabotages + the emergency button so mid-game stretches have events.
8. **Hygiene**: strip validator husks (`[invalid accusation target …]`, `[under-gate … redirected]`) from free_text; keep suspicion arithmetic out of rationales; pluralise the persona for 2-impostor games; fix `p-6 are your fellow saboteurs`.
