## 7. What is genuinely good

Recorded because a fair review needs both halves. Every item below is `[V]` unless marked.

**Determinism & correctness engineering**
1. **The replay contract is the best decision in the codebase.** `record_tick` stores actions + a hash, not state, so a 47-tick 9p2i replay is 557 kB and reconstruction is a real, self-verifying engine re-walk. `verify_samples.sh` re-walks and hash-verifies **all 100 committed replays in 3.14 s / 137 MB**.
2. **Determinism is designed in, not asserted.** Sorted iteration at every fan-out; content-free coordinate ids; pure copy-and-return belief folds; `math.fsum` with the hazard documented; integer cross-multiplication for a 6/7 threshold; `EngineRng` TRAINING_FAST correctly refused on any replay-writing construction. Verified independently: 3 seeds × 400 ticks byte-identical under two `PYTHONHASHSEED` values; run-twice replay *and* audit JSONL `cmp`-clean; 300-game / 28 362-tick fuzz with 0 violations across 12 invariants; 3 000-example Hypothesis probes over the meeting guard chain and the contradiction detector with zero invariant failures.
3. **Immutability is real.** `WorldState.__post_init__` wraps every mapping in `MappingProxyType(dict(...))` and tests prove in-place mutation raises. Rejections-as-events: `ActionRejectedError` becomes an event; hard `ValueError`s are reserved for state corruption.
4. **The observation boundary is a genuine chokepoint.** One privileged object, engine-free frozen `extra="forbid"` schemas, every collection a sorted tuple. Witness gates read only *resolved* engine events, so a rejected kill/vent cannot leak intent; `own_kill` is `event.actor == agent_id` only. A 16-mutation probe confirmed every classic channel (roles, cooldown, fellow ids, own_kill, kill/vent witness permission, in_vent, owner-scoped task ids) bites.
5. **The firewall is enforced three ways, not one** — an import-linter contract, an AST *source* scan that catches a planted import inside a function, and a recursive packet walker over Hypothesis games. Verified: no `agents/` module reaches `engine/` directly, transitively, via `TYPE_CHECKING`, or via `importlib`; no numpy/torch anywhere under `agents/`.
6. **Zero runtime import cycles** across 130 modules / 674 local edges. The 8 logical cycles that exist are all deliberately broken with `TYPE_CHECKING` guards or function-local imports.

**Gates and tooling**
7. **`mypy --strict` is honest.** `strict = true`, no per-module relaxations, **zero `type: ignore` in any production package** (all 140 live in `tests/`, where `warn_unused_ignores` keeps them fresh). 354 files clean in 0.58 s warm.
8. **`tests/meetings/test_prompt_byte_golden.py` is the single most valuable test in the repo** — it re-runs the *real* `MeetingManager` over 204 committed meetings against recorded prompt bytes, explicitly refuses to re-implement the assembly ("a second source of truth and a dishonest golden"), and ships `test_one_byte_template_perturbation_breaks_the_golden`: *"a golden that cannot fail is not a gate."* 7 s.
9. **Every gate proves it can fail.** The plant-detect-cleanup pattern (`test_numpy_torch_source_scan_rejects_planted_import`, `test_learned_package_scan_rejects_planted_import`, `test_verify_sh_detects_corruption`) is the right instinct — only its *location* is C-34.
10. **`scripts/check_doc_facts.py` runs against the real repository inside the normal gate** (`assert check_facts(_REPO_ROOT) == []`), re-deriving README provenance, win rates and the lever registry from committed bytes. ## 8. Cross-cutting reading

Three root causes generate most of the P1/P2 list:

- **"Preserve the exact prior behaviour" applied so strictly that the cleanup's benefit is cancelled.** Lever graduations delete the env read but keep the function, the parameter, the `if`, the `ENV_*` export and the tests (C-64, C-104). The walker consolidation unified seven mechanics but froze seven validation semantics into a 13-flag matrix (C-37). Both cost more than they saved.
- **Byte-identity doctrine freezing legibility bugs.** The prompt-byte golden plus committed baselines pin every render byte, so C-10, C-15, C-29 and the reported-testimony banding (C-73) all require a real-LLM re-record. That is the right doctrine for *substrate* changes and the wrong tax on pure legibility fixes; a render-version stamp would decouple them.
- **An architectural constraint satisfied by copying rather than by relocating the shared code.** `agents ↛ training` is a sound rule (numpy's BLAS reduction order is not bit-stable), but the fix was a 969-line verbatim fork (C-33) rather than putting the numpy-free logic in the package `training/` is already allowed to import.

And one measurement worth restating: the repo is **95 824 lines of process narration (`agent_prompts/` + `tasks/` + `audits/`) against 57 776 lines of core product Python (1.66:1)**, with 3 358 lines of durable engineering docs (0.8 %). The process tree is a genuine asset for understanding *why*, but there is no short path from "I need to change X" to "here is the one audit that explains X" other than the in-code references that C-63 asks to trim.
