# Evidence-use experiment scorecard

This is the measurement plan for `tasks/work/reasoning-evidence-experiments.md`.
The existing three-lever slate remains FINDING: its reporter-share bar missed
at 11/20 against <0.40. Neither implementation nor this offline scorecard
adopts a candidate. Existing slate-OFF and slate-ON bytes remain compatibility
controls when the new profile is absent.

## Populations and cases

The committed canonical and ML recording sets are a historical diagnostic
population. Report their complete file/meeting counts and exact input hashes;
do not describe hand-picked bad examples as held-out evidence. Recorded
ballots stay fixed, so rendering/reduction ablations cannot claim new outcomes.

Development cases use the previously identified late-discovery, one-edge and
two-edge travel, duplicate movement/endpoint, marker-injection and lost-origin
shapes. Held-out engineering controls are predefined transformations: renamed
players, shifted ticks, a disconnected/custom topology, another independent
witness, interrupted sighting spans and a different memory budget. They test
generalization of deterministic mechanics, not unseen model behavior.

| Mechanism | Positive case | Adverse/control case | Required measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Death versus discovery | Last alive, public dead announcement, later discovery | No last-alive or announcement; hidden kill tick differs | Only entitled bounds; no inferred exact death time |
| Travel | One edge/one tick and two edges/two ticks | Insufficient time, disconnected room, unknown placement | Correct feasibility; a feasible route never asserts innocence |
| Witness identity | Two independent witnesses | One repeated witness or movement plus its endpoint | Exact independent-source counts |
| Detector strength | Strong contradiction and deliberate weak rule | Untrusted text containing the weak-marker spelling | Explicit candidate band; historical marker behavior preserved |
| Memory | Movement origin and source identity survive reduction | Missing source, hearsay, gaps, coalescing, tight budget | Retained versus eligible facts and attribution; losses named |
| Additional reply | New structured charge after target already spoke | Repetition, no new charge, dead target, timeout/cancellation | At most one extra reply/call; actual usage and latency |
| Ballot intent | Authored target agrees with final outcome | Guard rewrites the target | Count observed outcome separately from voluntary model intent |

Proof and no-proof rows remain separate. Certified vent deduction is an
intended mechanism, not evidence of general accusation quality. Abstention is
reported separately from correct and wrongful ejection. Baseline co-presence
rules and weak narrow-window treatment remain controls unless a distinct
measured candidate changes them.

## Output and limits

One source-bound JSON report records profile versions, source hashes, exact
case and corpus denominators, per-case pass/fail, memory retention/attribution,
duplicate counts, fixed recorded outcomes, and additional reply calls, tokens
and latency. Zero eligible rows means undefined, never a success or zero error.
Repetition and retained evidence are counted semantically rather than by text
length. Costs from recorded attempts remain accounted independently of valid
outcomes.

Use deterministic scripted providers only. The offline ceiling is 40 meeting
fixtures, 300 historical files and no provider network calls. Future model
evaluation needs an authorized provider/token/cost/wall budget and a separately
preregistered quality test with new responses; this scorecard cannot supply it.
