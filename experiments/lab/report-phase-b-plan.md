## Phase B — Deduction/Information rework so the repaired rubric becomes MEASURABLE

### What "success" means (rubric-anchored, verified)
The committed baseline (`experiments/lab/results-rubric-score.json`, git_head 1e48c40) shows the exact hole Phase B must fill:
- **R7 strong-evidence meeting share = 0/114 (0%)** — every one of the 112 flags is a WEAK `alibi_vs_sighting` (self-stated). `r7_legible: 0.0` in all 50 per-game rows. This is the dark term.
- **R3** arcs already land in some games (`r3_arcs: 1.0` on ~9 seeds; close-audit reconstructs 15/39 carry-driven ejections) but only via Rule-1 body-proximity; R3 has no *growing* supply of persistent physical signal.
- **R1** eject-driven win share = 6/50; the headline lever for "deduction, not the clock."

Phase B succeeds iff a **re-extraction** of the committed 9p2i replays moves R7 off 0 with STRONG flags that name TRUE impostors (not crewmates), and a final combined re-record shows R3/R1 rising without R4 (wrong-ejections / threshold-inversions, HARD floor) regressing.

### The decisive correction to the inputs
All three original designs lead with either a `same_room_only` base flip or a belief-spread magnitude change. The adversarial probes refute both as the *spine*:
1. **The base flip is a net LOSS.** `inference_feasibility_probe.py` (committed report): adjacency 65% killer-recall → room_only 26% → room+breadcrumb 34%. The "34% floor" the brief cites IS the breadcrumb arm — 31 points BELOW today. And `visibility_resim.py`: room_only moves impostor wins 11→5, kills 165→141 — it re-blinds the impostor's own `_scored_targets` (`agents/tactical/impostor_policy.py:937-987` reads the same visibility-gated sighting stream as the crew). Room-local is a *balance lever*, not a free recall trade.
2. **The probe's breadcrumb is omniscient/crew-collective** (`inference_feasibility_probe.py:100-107`: `any(snap.get(c)==R for c in crew_alive)` over per-tick truth), NOT the firsthand single-witness 2-row pattern the B1 design specifies — so 34%/0.28 is an *upper bound*, not the realized firsthand floor.
3. **Testimony-ingestion is the wrong layer and inert on R7.** R7 (`rubric_score.py:345-388 _meeting_has_strong_impostor_flag`) keys ONLY on a contradiction row carrying `strong=True`; a testimony "voice" is a sighting that *backs* an accusation, never an alibi-clash, so it mints no contradiction. Converting two-witness meetings to ejections moves R1/R3, never R7.

### The spine: a meeting-time INFERENTIAL detector that is offline-testable on committed replays
The unlock is structural and was verified in code: **the extractor re-runs the detector over the recorded transcript.** `audits/workflows/extract_gameplay_facts.py:358` does `for flag in detect_contradictions(transcript, roster=roster)` and stamps `"strong": not is_weak_contradiction(contra)` (:286-293). `is_weak_contradiction` is purely a description-marker check (`meetings/transcript.py:562`). **Therefore a new contradiction class emitted WITHOUT the weak marker lights R7 on a pure RE-EXTRACTION of the already-committed replays — $0, zero re-record, fully honoring the cadence doctrine's "one combined re-record at the end."** This is the cheapest possible Phase-B gate and it is the spine.

### CHOSEN designs (folding in the mitigations that held)

**(1) Inferential detector — CHOSEN, the headline lever.** Add new STRONG contradiction classes derived at meeting time from the PUBLIC transcript's reconstructed positions (never perception-time, never engine state — preserving the firewall):
- **B2 cross-speaker alibi_conflict promoted STRONG.** Two different non-subject speakers place the same subject in two rooms over overlapping ticks. Already not-self-stated; its only weak markers today are adversarial/self-pair/narrow/boundary (`transcript.py:412-416`). KEEP those guards verbatim (the adversarial guard exists because seed-13 m2's impostor weaponised a counter-alibi); promote ONLY genuinely-independent cross-speaker conflicts. This is `report-grounding-audit.md` P1 ("add an inferential path").
- **B3/B4 last-seen-with-victim / unaccounted-during-kill, MEETING-TIME ONLY.** Reconstruct positions from everyone's publicly *stated* sightings in the transcript (the `inference_feasibility_probe.reconstruct` logic promoted to a pure transcript helper) and emit `alibi_vs_physical` STRONG when an alibi is physically impossible given the stated sightings. **MITIGATION THAT HELD (firewall):** the perception-time `LAST_SEEN_DELTA`/`BREADCRUMB_DELTA` forms in the original design are DROPPED — GlobalView (`observation/packet.py:106-123`) exposes no per-player liveness channel, so "V never seen alive after t by anyone" is not firewall-constructible and a leak test does not even scan the belief layer. All "absence/last-seen" inference lives in the meeting layer over public testimony only.

**CONSTRAINED:** every new STRONG flag must stay in the corroborate-within-round band — do NOT grant the full single-signal `CONTRADICTION_SUSPICION_DELTA=0.3` (`beliefs.py:50`) to a lone reconstruction atom (that re-opens the 13/13 wrong-ejection path the weak delta closed, `beliefs.py:54-73`). Reserve full-0.3 ONLY for a genuine TWO-source conjunction (uncorroborated-claim AND independent physical placement); a single inferential atom emits at the weak/mid band so it INFORMS but cannot eject alone (the `+0.05 < 0.10` gate-distance invariant, `beliefs.py:157-163`). Keep `contradiction_lift_key` dedup + `MEETING_CONTRADICTION_LIFT_CAP=0.3` (`beliefs.py:75,583`) so flags can't stack to 1.0.

**(2) Visibility — DEFERRED behind the detector; do NOT flip the base now.** The probe shows the firsthand breadcrumb floor is unmeasured and the omniscient version (34%) is below today. **OPEN/owner call.** If room-local ships at all, it is a SEPARATE wave with a fake-provider impostor-kill-rate sweep first (it re-blinds the impostor) and owner balance sign-off (unsanctioned balance lever, same class as the frozen clock). The detector spine does NOT need a visibility change — it runs on today's `same_room_and_adjacent` 65%-recall feed, which is the higher-recall substrate.

**(3) Testimony-ingestion — CHOSEN but SECOND, scoped to R1/R3 not R7.** The graduated voice-count spread (1 voice +0.05 unchanged sub-gate; 2 independent voices +0.12 first gate-cross; persist only the flat +0.05) is sound belief-fold engineering and correctly preserves every anti-cascade guard. **MITIGATION THAT HELD:** it is a no-op where voices don't exist, and it does NOT move R7 (separate channel). Sequence it AFTER the detector so it amplifies a now-richer evidence supply, and gate it on R1/R3 CONVERSION explicitly NOT the win-split (decoupled, `rubric_score.py:447-450`) and NOT R7. Keep `TESTIMONY_INDEPENDENCE_BAR=2`; persist only +0.05 (no cross-round railroad).

**(4) Prompts — CHOSEN minimal, two risky pieces split out.** Ship the breadcrumb RENDER (`store.py:602-655` directional "saw X leave A→R" from existing episodic deltas — pure, firewall-clean, no packet field) + the "others' accounts are belief-movers, second observation-backed contradictions" framing + the trim. **MITIGATIONS THAT HELD:** (a) the in-band `reasoning` field is HIGH-risk (the seed-35/seed-30 husk failures at `manager.py:2070,2104-2113`; MeetingTurn is `extra='forbid'` frozen and `model_copy(update=)` cannot delete it from the record) — make it a parse-only throwaway sub-schema gated behind its own offline qwen length/husk validation on the deception battery, NOT a 4-site strip; (b) do NOT delete the anti-narration patches until the offline harness proves the reasoning field contains deliberation rather than duplicating it into free_text.

- **CONSTRAINED (engine/firewall):** no perception-time absence inference (no liveness channel); all new flags meeting-time over public transcript; witness/self/roster guards in `beliefs.py:757-799` unchanged; gate 0.60, SKIP first-class, `guard_ballot_target_graph` untouched.
